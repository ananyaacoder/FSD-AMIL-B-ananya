Rani Lakshmibai, the Queen of Jhansi, was a prominent leader and symbol of resistance during the Indian Revolt of 1857 against British rule. Born Manikarnika Tambe in Varanasi, she was trained in martial arts and horse riding from a young age, married the Maharaja of Jhansi, and fought bravely to defend her kingdom after it was targeted for annexation by the British East India Company. Though she died in battle in 1858, she is remembered as a courageous warrior and an enduring icon of India's struggle for independence.
