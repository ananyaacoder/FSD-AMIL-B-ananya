Lata Mangeshkar (born Hema Mangeshkar on September 28, 1929, in Indore) was an iconic Indian playback singer and music composer, widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential singers of the Indian subcontinent, earning her titles like "Queen of Melody" and "Voice of the Millennium". With a career spanning over seven decades, she recorded thousands of songs in numerous Indian languages and contributed significantly to Hindi cinema's music industry, eventually becoming a member of the Parliament of India. She passed away on February 6, 2022, leaving behind a legendary legacy.
Birth and Family: Hema Mangeshkar was born to Deenanath Mangeshkar, a classical singer and theatre actor, and Shevanti. Father's Influence: Her father, a respected musician, recognized and nurtured her exceptional singing talent from a young age. Early Career: Mangeshkar began her career in the 1940s, initially performing in Marathi films before transitioning to Hindi cinema.
